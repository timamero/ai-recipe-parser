# Golden Curry Beef

**Prep Time**: 10 min

**Cook Time**: 30 min

**Servings**: 4

**Estimated Calories**: Estimated 546 calories per serving.

---

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1** large onion
- [ ] **2** small/medium tomatoes
- [ ] **1 lb** ground beef
- [ ] **3 cubes** Golden Curry
- [ ] **1 cup** water
- [ ] **to taste** optional seasonings: soy sauce & garlic powder

## Instructions
0. Chop the onion and tomatoes.
1. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned, about 5-7 minutes.
2. Add the chopped onion and tomatoes to the skillet and sauté until the onion is translucent, about 3-5 minutes.
3. Add the water and Golden Curry cubes to the skillet, stirring until the cubes are dissolved.
4. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Season to taste with soy sauce and garlic powder. Serve hot.
