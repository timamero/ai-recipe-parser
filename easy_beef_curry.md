# Easy Beef Curry

**Prep Time**: 10 min (estimate)

**Cook Time**: 30 min (estimate)

**Servings**: 3

**Estimated Calories: 546 per serving

---

## Ingredients

- [ ] **1** large onion
- [ ] **2** small/medium tomatoes
- [ ] **1 lb** ground beef
- [ ] **3 cubes** Golden Curry
- [ ] **1 cup** water
- [ ] **to taste (optional)** soy sauce
- [ ] **to taste (optional)** garlic powder

## Instructions
0. Chop the onion and tomatoes.
1. In a pan, sauté the onion until translucent.
2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
3. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and cook until softened.
4. Add the water and Golden Curry cubes, stirring until dissolved.
5. Simmer for about 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Add optional seasonings like soy sauce and garlic powder to taste.
7. Serve hot, optionally with rice. The preparation time is an estimate.
